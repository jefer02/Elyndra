A cleaner, brighter Elyndra: the game art now shows through clearly in both light and dark mode.

## What's new
- **Clean hero art:** no more dark bands or shadows over the background. Titles and descriptions stay white with a subtle, tight shadow, so the art looks clear in light and dark mode.
- **Meridian, redesigned:** the wheel shows only the game icons on a slimmer rail, so you see more of the art. Light mode uses a very transparent curved glass that follows the wheel.
- **New setting:** Settings → Appearance → **List background**: *Glass* or *Fog*, in both themes.
- **Sharper logos** in the classic layout, drawn like Meridian's. They're better sized: smaller in the library, bigger inside folders.
- **Portrait mode:** long titles shrink to fit in up to two lines and logos no longer take over the screen.
- **Light-mode controls** (tabs, sort, counter) use a light glass look that matches the theme.
- **Fixed:** some Android game descriptions started with a repeated heading (e.g. "CREA Crea…").
- The A–Z letter strip next to the Meridian list was removed.
