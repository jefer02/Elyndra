Meet Lucy: Elyndra's companion has a new name, a new look, and you can now simply talk to her.

## What's new
- **Lucy:** the companion is now called Lucy everywhere in the app, with a new emblem.
- **Live voice:** talk to Lucy out loud and she answers. You can cut in while she's speaking, she keeps listening through pauses, and the microphone lock is remembered. A clear sign shows when she can't hear you, and mute is respected.
- **Lucy front and centre:** her screen is built around her, with subtitles, short replies and recommendations shown as cards you can open.
- **She acts when you ask:** say what you want, or just say yes, and Lucy does it right away.
- **Trust that grows:** Lucy's relationship with you has levels, shown as a badge you can tap to see how it grows.
- **Picks up where you left off:** Lucy remembers your last conversation.
- **Ports from the store:** Lucy can recommend native ports and install them for you.
- **Quieter notifications:** Lucy only notifies you about things that matter, and device warnings appear only when they're relevant.
- **Fixed:** play time was sometimes saved as zero minutes with emulators that open the game from their own menu; a rescan could drop games from a folder that failed to load; the app could close when launching a PC game or copying a file into a folder that was no longer reachable; the one-time art upgrade could keep running in the background without finishing.
- **Fixed:** Lucy interrupting herself or closing the app while you talk, and empty stats on her screen.
